Feverguard T31

Temperature

Feverguard T31 is a wearable thermometer. It is worn on the body and sends continuous readings to an app on your phone, so you can follow a child's temperature through the night without waking them.

Stock Feverguard
37.4°C
Time07:00

Illustrative reading, not patient data

Made for the nights you would otherwise sit up

  • Continuous

    Readings are taken through the night and shown in the app, so a sleeping child stays asleep.

  • ±0.1 °C

    Stated measurement accuracy of the T31 sensor.

  • Worn anywhere

    Designed to measure from different areas of the body, including the underarm.

  • Every age

    Made with babies and young children in mind and suitable for the whole family.

  • Built to last

    A reusable device with replaceable adhesive bands, sold in packs of 15.

Feverguard T31 measures and displays body temperature. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any illness. If you are worried about a child's temperature, contact NHS 111 or your GP. Sold in the UK under the IME-DC & Orkinos brand.
